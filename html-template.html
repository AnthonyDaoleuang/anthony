Services Offered

The Programming and Web Development shop at LVTI can create promotional websites for school events and community activities. These websites help increase awareness and participation by providing important information in one easy website.

Goods And Services We Provide

  • Website design and development
  • Event promotion websites
  • Online event information pages
  • Digital Advertising for school events
  • Contact pages for organizers and volunteers
  • Event schedules and descriptions

Example

A website could be created for SkillsUSA event that include the event date, time, location, activies, and contact information for organizers. This would help the event gain more attention and participation.

How This Benefits the Community

Our websites help students, parents, staff, businesses, and residents stay informed about events happening at LVTI.

Local businesses and organizations benefit because more people learn about community events and opportunities. Event organizers gain better promotion, which can increase attendance and community involvement.

Residents benefit by knowing when events start and end, learning how they can participate, and staying connected with activities taking place in the community.
